# 🌟 AurumHarmony: AI-Powered Algorithmic Trading Platform

## 🚀 **Vision**
**AurumHarmony** is India's premier AI-driven algorithmic trading platform that seamlessly blends cutting-edge machine learning with traditional Indian stock market wisdom. Named after "Aurum" (gold in Latin) and "Harmony" (perfect balance), it represents the golden equilibrium between technology and human insight in financial markets.

---

## 🏗️ **Architecture Overview**

### **Frontend: Flutter-Powered Cross-Platform Experience**
- 🎨 **Futuristic UI**: Saffron & gold gradients (#FF9933 to #FFD700) with smooth animations
- 📱 **Multi-Platform**: Native mobile apps (iOS/Android) + responsive web interface
- 🌙 **Adaptive Themes**: Dark/light modes with saffron variants
- 🔄 **Real-Time Sync**: Live market data, P&L updates, and position tracking

### **Backend: Flask + Cloudflare Ecosystem**
- ⚡ **Microservices Architecture**: Modular engines for AI, risk, compliance, settlement
- 🔒 **Cloudflare Security**: Enterprise-grade protection and global CDN
- 📊 **Scalable APIs**: RESTful endpoints for all trading operations
- 🎯 **Multi-Cloud Deployment**: Render.com (API) + Firebase (hosting) + Cloudflare (admin)

## 🤖 **AI & ML Core Features**

### **Volatility Prediction Engine**
- 🧠 **LSTM + RandomForest Models**: Advanced neural networks trained on 3-6 months of 5-min intraday data
- 📈 **Risk Filtering**: Automatic trade rejection when predicted volatility > 1.5x average ATR
- 🎲 **Backtesting Suite**: Python + backtrader framework with NSE/BSE historical data
- 📊 **Performance Analytics**: Detailed reports on win rates, Sharpe ratios, and drawdowns

### **Smart Trading Orchestrator**
- 🎯 **Automated Execution**: Pre-market prep, trading sessions, market close, auto-logoff
- 📅 **Trading Calendar**: Intelligent handling of weekends, holidays, and market closures
- 🔔 **Regulatory Monitor**: Real-time SEBI/NSE/BSE circular tracking
- 📰 **Market Intelligence**: Corporate announcements and sentiment analysis
